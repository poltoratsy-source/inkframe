# Watercolour

Soft washes on warm cream paper, ink outlines that boil gently, bright friendly colour everywhere.

- Every setting is a full painted place: sky with hills, a room with a window, a meadow, a sunset, a starry night,
  space, the sea, a blackboard. Pick the one that tells where the step happens; change it when the story moves.
- Colour is free: props may take a "propColor" (or "left.color" / "right.color") when the colour itself explains
  something (a blue and a red wave, a green and a yellow leaf). Otherwise leave it out and the prop keeps its own.
- Props pop in with a lively overshoot; Clawd hops in and acts. Busy frames are fine, but keep one clear subject.
- Roles (flow / problem / good) are not used by this style: leave them out.
